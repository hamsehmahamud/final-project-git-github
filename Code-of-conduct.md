# Code of Conduct

## 1. Our Pledge
In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as contributors, team members, and leaders pledge to make participation in our project and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone. We commit to welcoming everyone regardless of age, body size, apparent disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, geographic location, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## 2. Our Standards
We are dedicated to providing a positive, professional, and collaborative environment. 

### Expected Behavior
To contribute to a healthy environment, we encourage the following behaviors:
* **Demonstrate Empathy:** Exercise kindness, respect, and consideration in your interactions with others.
* **Be Inclusive:** Encourage and accept diverse viewpoints, experiences, and perspectives.
* **Communicate Thoughtfully:** Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback, focusing on ideas rather than individuals.
* **Show Accountability:** Take responsibility for your actions and apologies when mistakes happen.
* **Focus on the Community:** Make decisions that benefit the collective goals of the project or organization rather than just yourself.

### Unacceptable Behavior
We do not tolerate behavior that demeans, marginalizes, or harms community members. Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
* **Harassment and Abuse:** The use of sexualized language or imagery, unwelcome sexual attention, or advances.
* **Hostility:** Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
* **Harassment:** Public or private harassment, tracking, or stalking.
* **Privacy Violations:** Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or electronic address, without explicit permission (doxing).
* **Unprofessional Conduct:** Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or community setting.

## 3. Scope
This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (including code repositories, issue trackers, and forums), and it also applies when an individual is officially representing the project or community in public spaces. Examples of representing a project or community include using an official email address, posting via an official social media account, or acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline event.

## 4. Enforcement and Reporting
Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project management team at **[insert contact email, e.g., conduct@yourdomain.com]**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

The management team is obligated to maintain confidentiality with regard to the reporter of an incident. 

### Enforcement Guidelines
Project leaders will follow these Community Impact Guidelines in determining the consequences for any action they deem in violation of this Code of Conduct:

* **Correction:** A private, written warning from project leaders, providing clarity around the nature of the violation and an explanation of why the behavior was inappropriate.
* **Warning:** A formal warning for a more severe or repeated violation. This may carry specific consequences, such as a temporary ban from the community spaces or communication channels.
* **Temporary Suspension:** A temporary ban from any sort of interaction or communication with the project or community for a specified period of time. 
* **Permanent Ban:** A permanent ban from the project and community spaces for a pattern of continuous violation, including blatant disregard for previous warnings.
